Failure is part of the contract

A bad delivery leaves the good report available.

The recorded exercise rejects missing files, changed bytes, duplicate identities, missing revisions, out-of-order deliveries, foreign currency, inconsistent statement amounts and interruption before promotion. Each rejection emits a visible exception and preserves the published report.

The receiver uses one sequential writer, fixed simulated collections and cumulative source deliveries. Checksums detect drift against a trusted expected digest; they are not signatures.
