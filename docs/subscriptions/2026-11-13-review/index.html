      Definitions that prevent bad decisions

      MRR
      Month-end contractual recurring run rate. No usage fees, proration, foreign exchange, free plans or annual billing normalization.
      Billed amount
      Latest visible invoice face value by issue month. A revised invoice replaces the earlier version at later knowledge cutoffs.
      Net cash
      Payments by paid date minus refunds by refunded date. Collection timing can differ from invoice timing.
      Bookings
      Not calculated. A signed contract's total commitment is not established by these monthly subscription events.
      Recognized revenue
      Not calculated. Performance obligations and recognition schedules are absent. This is not an accounting standard implementation.
      Balance and exposure
      Invoice face value − payments + refunds. Negative values are credits; they do not erase another invoice's unpaid amount. Period-end gross unpaid and credits are shown separately. Due dates and credit-note policy are absent, so unpaid does not imply overdue or collectible. This is a simplified invoice ledger, not a general ledger.
