Limits. Public practice data for one UK retailer, not data from any employer. The product table follows the period only; it is not split by country or customer type. Active customers are counted per month and cannot be added across months, so the facts row shows a monthly average. Return rate excludes returns that exactly mirror a sale by the same customer within an hour (order-entry reversals); the as-reported rate is shown beside it.
